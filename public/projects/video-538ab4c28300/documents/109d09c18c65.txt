【剧情梗概（完整叙事）】

第一幕 · 仇人相见（00:00–00:29）｜场景002 建筑工地
Cathy 怒掴 Vincent（Mr. Wallace），保镖怒喝"How dare you hit Mr. Wallace!""Bitch, do you want to die?"。承包商 Samuel 赶来自报家门："Mr. Wallace, I'm the site manager of this project, Samuel." Cathy 认出这就是撞父仇人，厉声索还："So you're Samuel, return my dad's heart and money!" Samuel 劝她"别冲动"。此时 Vincent 突然旧疾发作（"Vincent's having another episode"），被搀扶离场，临走冷冷下令："Issue a billion dollars reward to find the woman with a floral birthmark."

第二幕 · 讨薪受辱（00:29–02:16）｜场景002 建筑工地
Vincent 留下"Handle this properly"，Samuel 谄媚应下。Cathy 当众讨要父亲手术费："pay up! At least give me $200,000 first... My dad's waiting for surgery!" Samuel 阴阳怪气："Since Mr. Wallace told me to handle it, of course I will."随即翻脸——"What he meant was dealing with you!" Cathy 愤而将东西砸向他（"Samuel, this is for you!"）。工友们涌上声援"They're bullying Kathy!"，Samuel 威胁"Planning a rebellion?""all of your salary will be confiscated!""I'll fucking kill you!"，并禁止她再在工地摆摊。工友 Benny 无奈致歉："We're still counting on Samuel to pay us our salary, we can't help you anymore."众人凑钱相赠，Cathy 含泪拒收："I can't take your money... I'll find a way to get it."工友劝她别再来了，Cathy 立誓救父。

第三幕 · 霸总寻人（02:20–02:41）｜场景003 豪车内部 / 场景005 豪宅客厅
Vincent 吩咐手下查"刚才那女孩"的资料："Give me the info of that girl just now."手下打趣："Wow, bro. You've been single for 30 years. It's time to find someone."

第四幕 · 豪门催婚 · 母亲登门（02:41–03:16）｜场景005 豪宅客厅
手下回禀："Vincent, I've found 20 heiresses. Each with a floral birthmark. You can try them one by one."镜头转向豪宅：Vincent 电话里对母亲说"这周公司忙，不回家了，你自己打车回来"便挂断。Mrs. Wallace 怒极："Hello? Hello? This brat dares to hang up on me!"她与女仆商议 Vincent 的病情，当机立断："Since he won't come home, let's send them to the firm and corner him!"

第五幕 · 复仇钩子（03:21–03:37）｜场景002 建筑工地 / 场景005 豪宅客厅
另一端，Cathy 决意反击："A top lawyer? He's definitely going to seek justice from my dad!"（请顶尖律师，替父亲讨回公道）——为下一集"灰姑娘法律反击豪门"埋下钩子。
